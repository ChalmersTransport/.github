## Research software

[Large-scale LBBD Optimization](https://github.com/parishwadomkar/Large-scale-LBBD-Optimization) — research software developed by Omkar Parishwad in the Urban Mobility Research Group at Chalmers University of Technology. See the [v1.0.0 release](https://github.com/parishwadomkar/Large-scale-LBBD-Optimization/releases/tag/v1.0.0) and the [associated article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.adapen.2026.100310).
